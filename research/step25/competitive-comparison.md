# Source-grounded comparison of approaches

Inspected public sources on 22–23 October 2026 through the connected GitHub tool. These are engineering comparisons, not verified winners. File blob hashes and license evidence are in artifacts/competitor-source-audit.json. Results below are reported by the projects unless explicitly marked as our executed experiment. No competitor implementation is copied or run against our benchmark.

| System | Inputs and technique | Training / evaluation evidence | Limits and lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| [MatchSense](https://github.com/VishalKJ-ai/matchsense/blob/main/src/matchsense/analytics/tactical.py) | Tracking plus event minute buckets; 18-minute baseline, 8-minute recent; phase filters, multiple families, persistence and cooldowns | [evaluate_shifts.py](https://github.com/VishalKJ-ai/matchsense/blob/main/scripts/evaluate_shifts.py) uses comeback scripted changes versus balanced nulls, seeds 7000+i, default six matches/storyline, 20-minute matching | More forgiving horizon and different simulator/features. Matching credits one onset but marks every eligible candidate as matched, so duplicates are not penalized as in ours. Tactical rules are not evidence of a trained tactical classifier. |
| [Matchday Crew](https://github.com/timsheldon1/matchday-crew/blob/main/src/analyst.js) | Deterministic event momentum, EWMA smoothing, pressure spells/high turnovers and prioritized triggers; rolling windows/cooldowns | README reports 100-match correlation r=0.48 and 72% direction accuracy; not independently reproduced here | Neither is intervention onset precision/recall. Useful insight prioritization avoids needing every story to identify a hidden policy. No root license found during inspection; source was inspected conceptually, not copied. |
| [The Dugout](https://github.com/lauramiro/the-dugout/blob/main/docs/evaluation.md) | Decision triggers, counterfactual branches and fatigue/score context; paired alternative actions | Project reports 31 seeds, 15 fit/16 test; 149 paired points, 74 test triggers; 0.74 correlation, 78.4% sign, 10.8% coverage for nominal 90% intervals | Trigger outcomes differ from change detection. Poor stated interval coverage matters even with promising direction. A hand-added story point is disclosed separately. Not evidence of calibrated tactic-onset detection. |
| [Beyond the Scoreline](https://github.com/mateotoniolo/beyond-the-scoreline/blob/main/docs/AGENTS_CREW.md) | Deterministic event statistics/momentum, analyst→narrator→checker→deterministic producer; agent tools and grounding | Source documents 19 moments, 16 insights before full time, 16 verified; timing measured on seed 11, model/service conditions stated | Numeric grounding and latency are not tactical-label accuracy. Its tracking is reconstructed event interpolation rather than our pinned engine's native movement. Product lesson: deterministic facts should gate narrative. |
| MatchPulse Step 2 | Event-only adjacent 8-minute rolling location/share detector; PH/ADWIN comparison | Executed old 32-family holdout: P .133, R .484, F1 .209; 2.625 null alerts/match; 417/417 numeric packs | Evidence accuracy alone did not imply detection accuracy. Old data is exploratory for this milestone. |
| MatchPulse Step 2.5 | Native allowlisted positions, comparable-context line geometry and event confirmation; sequential and small supervised ablations | Executed 24 development /16 validation /40 fresh held-out families, seven scenarios/family; strict one-credit matching at 10 and20 minutes | Distinguishing contribution is the observability experiment, documented negative pressure result, opaque causal replay and complete numerical evidence. Final metrics and limitations are in detector-comparison.md and final-verdict.md. |

## Leakage and license inspection

MatchSense's evaluation passes a StoredMatch containing truth into `analyse`. Source inspection of `analytics/engine.py::analyse` (blob d9c78205c21cb1541e53ab9d5be46edf1476336b) and `live/feed.py::replay` (49e7a50b01f17c83fe43421bb4224d8d4e50f075) shows analytics initialized with metadata and replay consuming tracking/events, with no truth read on that inspected path. This is source evidence, not a complete security audit. Our interface is narrower: inference has no such truth-bearing container.

MatchSense, The Dugout and Beyond declare MIT at inspected roots; Matchday Crew had no root LICENSE. MIT permits reuse subject to notices, but this implementation is original and retains only factual/conceptual comparisons. Existing upstream football-simulator attribution remains unchanged. Project claims about Foundry, deployments and benchmarks are not capabilities of MatchPulse and are not adopted as validated facts.

Our differentiation is not a claim to have invented line-height metrics, rolling windows or agent fact checking. It is the tested path from native observable geometry to constrained numerical claims, with an explicit refusal to equate an observed line shift with a coach's high-press instruction.
