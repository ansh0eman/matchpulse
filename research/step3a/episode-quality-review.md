# Episode usefulness review

This is automated/developer inspection, not independent human review, and not a new untouched accuracy benchmark. The developer has already seen prior experiment classifications and cannot honestly claim blindness. A separate blinded packet is prepared in artifacts/review-packet.json (22 episodes); do NOT open review-key.json or evaluation.json before rating it. The key is for later reconciliation, not agent inference. Packet strips match UUIDs, filenames, scenarios, scripted onsets and external success/failure labels; intrinsic observed facts remain. Grouping/style may still reveal shared source episodes, so this is practical concealment rather than formal blinding.

Coverage: observed goals, shot attempts, conservative high gains, gain-to-shot sequences, repeated-shot activity, fresh approved position, stale suppressed position, a normal observed interval and a near7m comparison. The comparison is explicitly measurement-only: it does not prove persistence, fusion or eligibility and must not be called a verified story. Selected research cases include matched and unchanged episodes but that classification is withheld in packet. Compact raw event evidence and complete contributing frame geometry accompany eligible positional facts; suppressed case exposes support/age/before/after without presenting a claim. Human scores are null.

Preliminary developer findings: event score/count arithmetic and exact IDs reverify; fresh position qualifies, stale position does not. Shot incidents are now narrated once (richer sequence replaces attempt at the same source event); later outcomes remain inspectable instead of repeated cards. Gains still dominate the demo:45 independently narrated gains may be excessive for a fan broadcast despite a120s per-team cooldown. This is an editorial concern, not proof that their event arithmetic is false. No relevance thresholds were tuned to force a target story count. A quiet0–0 contains many routine gains and few major moments; the packet should decide whether to reduce this category in Step3B/UI.

No human relevance score, universal story accuracy, intervention precision or real-football calibration is claimed. Human review should precede calling the presentation broadcast-ready for judges. Narrow or disable categories that reviewers cannot interpret; do not invent narratives to fill silent minutes.
