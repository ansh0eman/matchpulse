## 2. One editorial owner, after causal publication

Add one pure frontend selector, `web/src/broadcast/editorial.ts`. `App.tsx` calls it on the result of `visibleSnapshot`, after the causal-prefix checks. Python continues to own factual eligibility, scheduling, compiled claims and independent proof. The new selector owns only UI prominence, history ordering and selection validity; no backend contract or scheduler change is proposed.

The selector receives the published `View | null`, requested scenario/clock, and an explicit selection reference. It returns:

- Full available scheduled history, passing source/availability/presentation clocks against **the published view clock**, in deterministic presentation-time/priority/ID order.
- `latestRecorded`: chronologically latest available story in the current audience scope, including boundaries. This is metadata, not automatic prominence.
- A timeline projection: retain the existing eight-row cap; keep an explicitly selected older row visible as one pinned additional row if necessary. No source records are deleted.
- A discriminated presentation: `loading`, `selected(story)`, `featured(story)`, `withheld(diagnostic)`, or `quiet`.
- Selection disposition: retain, clear, or pending during same-scope loading. Components must not independently rank stories or decide whether a positional diagnostic overrides them.

Keep these types in `editorial.ts`; only correct/complete the existing diagnostic shape in `contracts.ts`. Reuse Story IDs, claim IDs, overlay references/metrics, three clocks, and published match identity. No relevance score, model judgment, confidence value or new endpoint.

`BroadcastTheatre` renders this decision; `MatchTimeline` maps the supplied projection. `presentation.ts` formats existing verified values, without deciding eligibility. App's prominent studio/Ask actions use the same selected/featured story; history/recap remain available independently. Quiet/withheld states must not leave an unrelated enabled proof/Ask action elsewhere. Preserve existing disabled loading controls required by P4.

## 3. Proposed policy — provisional display choices

These rules are proposed product policy, not validated improvements in detector accuracy or human relevance.

1. **Explicit exploration wins.** A selected, still-available incident remains displayed regardless of age, automatic priority or diagnostic state. Selection pauses playback as today; it does not seek in Matchday. Proof uses that incident's claim IDs and original cutoff.
2. **One prominence window:** `FEATURE_WINDOW_SECONDS = 30` replay seconds, inclusive at 30, expired above 30. It is measured from `presented_seconds`, not wall time or latest tracking time. Rationale: a simple bounded reading interval that prevents indefinite narration; the value is provisional and unrelated to the gate's scientific freshness rules. Pausing pauses expiry. No minimum hold or additional cooldown is introduced.
3. **Automatic allowlist:** goal context, dismissal, regain-to-shot, eligible positional comparison, and recent attacking activity. Rank using their existing editorial order 100/95/80/70/50, then newer presentation time, then stable ID. Unknown types and missing priorities use the explicit type map; arbitrary incoming priority never promotes a routine type. A recent goal can hold attention over a newer routine event. Favorite team cannot suppress an opposition goal or change proof.
4. **Routine history:** observed shots, shot outcomes, high regains and match boundaries remain timeline items, with proof on intentional selection. Repeated routine events do not replace the main explanation or repeatedly announce themselves. Reuse scheduler deduplication, its 120s activity cooldown and the existing eight-row timeline cap; do not invent an “attacking dominance” aggregate. The recent list can still contain many shots; grouping/search is outside P6.2.
5. **Boundary reset:** a later available match-boundary event ends automatic prominence for incidents whose source time precedes that boundary. No new boundary-action field is needed. Half/full time remain available in history; a dedicated boundary banner is deferred. Never reset explicit, still-available history solely because a new boundary occurs.
6. **No featured candidate:** choose a recent suppressed positional review, if any, by newest `detected_at_seconds` then deterministic existing reason/time tie-break. Apply the same 30s display window measured from its review clock. Otherwise show quiet: “No new featured moment. Explore the recorded moments below.” Score, positions and replay controls continue to reflect the published prefix. Do not auto-open proof or scroll on automatic transitions.
7. **Withheld is a review status, not a match-wide verdict.** It never overrides a selected or feature-eligible verified incident. Diagnostics currently have no typed team association in the UI; do not use one diagnostic to revoke a verified claim for another team or declare that all positional comparisons are unavailable. Suppressed alerts and historical supported claims remain intact.

Fan and Analyst share the same prominence policy and factual identity. Fan gets a short team/action/comparison statement plus a plain qualification. Analyst gets the existing compiled detail and exact clocks/reasons; its existing high-regain timeline eligibility stays intact. Changing audience/favorite recompiles through the current API, not through invented frontend factual prose. Explicit selection persists only if the incident remains available in the new audience scope.

## 4. Temporal labels, comparison status and intentional selection

**Current replay state** is the visible prefix clock, score, period and original positions. **Latest recorded** is the latest available scheduled incident, which may be old or routine. **Selected historical** is a deliberate user choice. **Supported positional comparison** is an existing verified claim at its evidence cutoff, never a description of ongoing movement. **Withheld** is a particular recorded review with diagnostic reasons; **unavailable/quiet** has no inferred cause when diagnostics are absent.

Every featured or selected story displays its source, availability and presentation clocks. Always label automatic copy “Recorded moment”; selected copy remains “Selected past moment”. Use past-tense shot wording. A goal's old score is labeled as the recorded score after that incident, never presented as the current score; the scoreboard stays prefix-authoritative. A positional summary says “sampled positional comparison”, names the team and compared cutoff, and describes a three-deepest-outfielder proxy across sampled windows. It does not claim continuous advance/retreat, a back four, tactical instruction or current pressure effectiveness. Existing Proof Lens supplies windows, source samples and independent arithmetic.

For a suppressed diagnostic, use its actual reasons and clock: “At the recorded review at T, relevant evidence was A seconds old”, if A is present. Do not move that measurement to the replay clock. If showing age at replay instead, it must be explicitly derived from `latest_recent_seconds`, or recorded age + (published clock − review clock), with valid finite nonnegative inputs. Prefer the simpler review-time label for P6.2. Null age/span means unknown; no `nulls old`, fabricated coverage, tracking-outage explanation or current-proof button. Map known reason codes individually (stale, insufficient span/count, missing/unverified evidence); unknown reasons get a generic recorded-review limitation with raw reasons in Analyst detail. “Tracking observations available” refers only to `tracking_available`; it is not evidence of current coverage.

Store selection as `{scenario, matchId, storyId, eligibleAt}` where `eligibleAt = max(source, available, presented)` from the selected verified story. Display it only by resolving the ID in the new available list. A requested backward seek below `eligibleAt`, a scenario/match change, or a completed new view that excludes the selection permanently clears the stored reference. Same-scope loading is pending, not permission to show old proof or clear a still-valid selection. The selector returns this disposition; App applies it without a second policy. Render guards apply synchronously so effect timing cannot flash wrong/future proof. Once cleared, seeking forward must not restore selection. A still-available choice is never overridden by automatic expiry/ranking.
